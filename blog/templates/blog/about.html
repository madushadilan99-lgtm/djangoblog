About DjangoBlog

Welcome to DjangoBlog!

DjangoBlog is a blog website where users can share their thoughts, experiences, ideas, and stories.

Visitors can read published posts, explore different categories, and discover content shared by other users.

Registered users can log in, create their own posts, upload cover images, and manage their posts.

This website was built as a learning project using Django and web development technologies.
